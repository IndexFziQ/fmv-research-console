AniMaster: From Story Texts to Animated Videos via Cinematic Script Generation and Interactive Authoring

工具 影游生产
Ruiqi Yu, Dekun Qian, Jiale Xu, Sizhe Cheng, Yize Li, Xiangyang Wu, Zhiguang Zhou, Wei Chen, Yong Wang PDF ↗
Recent advances in Video Generation Models (VGMs) can produce short clips, but everyday creators still struggle to turn brief story texts into polished long-form animated videos. The authors identify two challenges of interactive video authoring: translating free-form story text into professional cinematic scripts and high-quality animation, and conveying video design intents (shot composition, camera controls, shot sequencing) to the model. Drawing on narratology and film studies, they propose a three-layer design framework (story texts / cinematic scripts / animated videos) and build AniMaster, a VGM-powered authoring tool that auto-expands stories into cinematic scripts, translates them into polished videos, and supports interactive script editing and video refinement. Validated via a 16-participant user study, two case studies, and interviews with film professionals.
核心问题：视频生成模型能出短片段，但普通创作者很难把简短故事文本变成专业、长篇、有电影感的动画视频——缺「故事→专业分镜脚本→高质量动画」的转化能力，也缺把视觉设计意图（构图、运镜、镜头顺序）传达给模型的手段。
解决方案：基于叙事学与电影研究提出三层设计框架（故事文本/分镜脚本/动画视频），做出 AniMaster 工具：自动把简短故事扩写成详细电影脚本，再按专业视觉叙事原则转成成片；支持文本指令+直觉交互编辑脚本、迭代视频。16 人用户研究+2 个案例+2 位电影专家访谈验证有效性与可用性。
对你有什么用：这就是互动影游生产管线的雏形——「文字剧本→电影脚本→视频」的自动转化+人工可编辑。影游做分支剧情/分镜，可借鉴它的三层抽象与「脚本中间层」：让模型出分镜、人来把关关键镜头意图，而不是黑盒直出成片。

CHARM: Character Hallucination for Multicultural Role Play Benchmark

硬数据 角色幻觉
Sunkyung Han, Nahyeon Park, Gaeun Seo, Seunghyun Yoon, JinYeong Bak PDF ↗
Role-playing LLMs should adopt a character's style while respecting that character's knowledge boundaries. Prior evaluations detect character hallucination but rarely distinguish whether errors arise from failing to recognize a boundary or from failing to comply despite recognition. CHARM is a multicultural benchmark of 40 real and fictional characters across five cultural-linguistic regions, validated by native reviewers. It probes Temporal (historical vs. modern) and Cross-Universe (entities outside a character's narrative universe) boundaries via abstention-enabled multiple-choice questions, separating Boundary-Awareness from Boundary-Compliance. Across six LLMs, hallucination is driven predominantly by compliance failures: models often acknowledge a query is out of scope yet still give factual, out-of-character answers (verified parametric overrides).
核心问题：角色扮演 LLM 要既模仿角色风格、又守住角色「知识边界」。现有评测能发现「角色幻觉」，却分不清错误是「没识别出边界」还是「识别了却没守住」——根因一直没定位清楚。
解决方案：CHARM 基准：40 个真实/虚构角色、5 个文化语言区、母语评审校验；测两类边界——时间性（历史 vs 现代）与跨宇宙（角色叙事宇宙外的实体），用可弃答选择题。两阶段评测把「边界意识」和「边界服从」分开。测 6 个模型发现幻觉主要由「服从失败」驱动：模型明知超出角色知识，仍给出事实性、出戏的回答（参数化覆盖），且存在系统性文化差异。
对你有什么用：影游角色「出戏/穿帮」的根因找到了——不是模型不知道边界，是「知道却守不住」。做角色知识边界（这个角色不该知道现代词/别的世界观设定），要显式加「弃答/装糊涂」约束，别指望模型自觉守住。

Disclosure-Gated User Simulation for Companion-Agent Evaluation

硬数据 陪伴代理
Yao Liu, Yu He PDF ↗
Using an LLM to play the user is standard in scalable evaluation, but the simulated user is excessively cooperative, so a system under test can score by the number of questions it asks rather than by making the user willing to speak. The authors introduce a disclosure gate that conditions information release on the companion agent's behavior: its state is a ladder of five ordered gates merged onto three observable depth layers, learned from a corpus's synthetic branch while the real branch supplies how people speak and react. On CompanionBench, once per-example gate labels are removed, the largest rank displacement across 12 systems exceeds the noise band, while per-system scores stay stable. Only the released simulator passes both acceptance criteria (order-preserving ranking and scale-stable scores), correlating at 0.993 with the benchmark's original simulator.
核心问题：用 LLM 模拟用户做规模化评测已成标配，但有个反复出现的毛病：模拟用户「过度配合」，被测系统可以靠「提问数量」刷分，而不是真正让用户愿意开口——评测失真，尤其对陪伴型代理。
解决方案：提出「披露门控」：把信息释放与陪伴代理的行为挂钩，内部是五级有序门控、合并成三层可观测深度；从语料合成支路学门控行为、真实支路学人们怎么说话与反应。在 CompanionBench 上，去掉逐例门控标注后，12 个被测系统的最大排名位移超出噪声带；作者只发布能同时满足「保序+尺度稳定」两条验收标准的模拟器，其榜单与原始模拟器相关性 0.993。
对你有什么用：影游做「情感陪伴/恋爱」角色，评测时别用「过度配合」的假玩家。真正要测的是角色能不能让玩家「愿意吐露」——「披露门控」把信息释放与角色行为绑定，能更真实地评估陪伴角色的吸引力。

Measuring the Behavioral Fidelity of Long-Horizon Human Activity Simulations

硬数据 行为保真
Yi Fei Cheng, Fan Yang, Iremsu Bas, Koichiro Niinuma, Narishige Abe, David Lindlbauer PDF ↗
As LLM-based human simulators are used for policy, evaluation, and training, they must faithfully reproduce real behavioral patterns. Prior work examined fidelity in survey responses and dialogue, but longer-horizon real-world activity remains unexplored. The authors introduce a framework for evaluating behavioral fidelity across temporal granularities and levels of analysis, collecting a 43-hour multi-camera dataset of in-the-wild office activity and comparing persona descriptors, few-shot exemplars, and statistical transition/time-of-day priors. Behavioral fidelity is not uniform: statistical priors bring activity and sequence distributions closest to real behavior, yet over-fragment routines and suppress within-person variability.
核心问题：LLM 人类模拟器越来越多用于策略/评测/训练，但「行为保真度」只在问卷和对话里被研究过，长时程真实活动几乎没人测——NPC 到底像不像真人做事，一直缺可靠度量。
解决方案：提出跨时间粒度、跨分析层级的长时程活动模拟保真度评测框架；采集 43 小时多机位办公室真实活动数据，对比「人设描述/少样本示例/统计转移+时段先验」三种条件机制。发现保真度不统一：统计先验让活动与序列分布最接近真实，却过度切碎日常、压制个体内变异性。
对你有什么用：影游要让 NPC 的「一天」像真人，单靠人设描述不够，要补统计先验（什么时间做什么事）。但统计先验会把角色变成机械作息——真人的「随机性/个体差异」也是保真度的一部分，评估要跨多个指标、多个时间粒度一起看。

VIBE-Bench: Evaluating Personalized Large Language Models When Profiles Don't Mean Preferences

硬数据 人设偏好
Yiwen Jiang, Yang Deng, Stephanie Fong, Zimu Wang, Yaling Shen, Wei Feng, Hongxi Yang, Xiangyu Zhao, Zhongxing Xu, Deval Mehta, Xuelian Cheng, Zongyuan Ge PDF ↗
Personalized LLMs (PLLMs) tailor responses to individual users, where the central challenge is preference reasoning. Existing benchmarks assume preference can be retrieved from semantically related history. The authors study profile-preference conceptual misalignment (PRCM), where observable profile cues and query-specific preferences lie in different concept spaces. VIBE-Bench contains two psychology-grounded tasks, 3,504 personas, and 12,239 dialogues with a manually verified gold test set, requiring cross-concept preference reasoning. Current PLLMs largely rely on shallow semantic correlations and fail to acquire robust cross-concept mappings.
核心问题：个性化 LLM 的核心是「偏好推理」，但现有基准假设「偏好能从语义相关历史检索到」。现实里「人设线索」和「具体偏好」常在不同概念空间（档案-偏好概念错位），语义检索会失效。
解决方案：VIBE-Bench 基准：两个基于心理学的任务、3504 个人设、12239 段对话，含人工校验黄金测试集，要求跨概念偏好推理。实验发现当前个性化方法大多靠浅层语义相关，拿不到稳健的跨概念映射。
对你有什么用：影游做角色个性化，别以为「档案写了什么」就等于「这角色会怎么选」。角色/玩家的显性人设和真实偏好常常错位——要做跨概念的偏好推理，而不是语义匹配，才能真正懂角色「嘴上说一套、心里想一套」。

Data-Driven Persona-Conditioned Agents for A/B Test Simulation

落地 用户画像
Ziyad Benomar, Weronika Łajewska, Leonardo Perelli, Saab Mansour PDF ↗
A/B testing is the gold standard for evaluating product changes, but each experiment requires real traffic, engineering effort, and weeks of measurement. The authors propose a simulation framework that predicts A/B test outcomes using LLM agents conditioned on data-driven personas grounded in real user behavioral signals (activity patterns, engagement signals, inferred demographics), unlike prior synthetic or rule-based personas. On a benchmark of 40 A/B tests spanning two metric types, the best configuration achieves 0.75-0.90 directional accuracy, demonstrating that data-driven personas are a viable path to fast, low-cost experiment pre-screening.
核心问题：A/B 测试是产品决策金标准，但每次都要真实流量+工程投入+数周测量，慢且贵；想提前毙掉烂方案却缺低成本手段。
解决方案：用 LLM 代理 + 数据驱动人格（来自匿名行为数据：活动模式/参与信号/推断人口统计）模拟 A/B 结果，不做合成/规则人设。40 个 A/B 测试、两类指标上，最好配置方向准确率 0.75-0.90。
对你有什么用：这是给「投放/运营」用的——影游上功能、改剧情分支、换文案，可以用「数据驱动的人格代理」低成本预筛 A/B，先把明显差的方案毙掉，再拿真流量验证。比拍脑袋快一个量级。

Making Prospective Memory SLM-Shaped: Typed Intention Stores for Small-Model Agents

架构 意图记忆
Jinqing Zhao, Chengcan Wu PDF ↗
Prospective memory means carrying out a deferred intention at the right future cue while other work continues. Benchmarks now isolate it as an agent skill, yet frontier LLMs still struggle: the best published PM-Bench scaffold reaches only 65.1% Set-F1. The authors argue this is schema-constrained state tracking rather than open-ended reasoning, and that small models can execute it when the action space is typed. They propose the Prospective Intention Store (PIS), putting lifecycle logic in code and scoped language work on the model, training-free and agentic. On PM-Bench, DeepSeek-Chat with PIS reaches 82.9% Set-F1, and small models surpass the published large-model scaffold.
核心问题：前瞻记忆（在正确未来线索触发时执行延迟意图）是 agent 关键技能，但前沿大模型也难：最佳 PM-Bench 脚手架只到 65.1% Set-F1——大模型硬记都吃力。
解决方案：论证这是「受模式约束的状态跟踪」而非开放推理，小模型在「动作空间类型化」后就能执行。提出前瞻意图存储 PIS：把生命周期逻辑放代码、把语言工作限定在模型上，训练自由、无需微调。DeepSeek-Chat+PIS 达 82.9%，小模型反超大模型脚手架。
对你有什么用：影游 NPC 要「记住玩家几章前说的事、在合适时机兑现」（伏笔回收），别指望大模型硬记。把「意图生命周期」做成代码状态机+类型化动作，小模型也能低成本、高可靠地兑现延迟承诺——这是可落地的 NPC 记忆架构。

Classic AI Scaffolding for LLM Social Agents

架构 社交剧本
Anatole Gershman PDF ↗
LLMs can produce locally plausible social turns, but fluent next-turn generation is not enough for social simulation. Human encounters such as restaurant lunches and hotel check-ins are bounded social episodes with roles, scripts, material state, obligations, commitments, timing, and closure conditions. The author presents EpisodeSim, a hybrid LLM-agent architecture representing classic-AI structures as natural-language control state interpreted by LLM calls, where a World Master maintains shared reality, constructs scenes, adjudicates proposed actions, tracks effects and obligations, and controls closure. Small qualitative ablations on two held-out settings support the claim that coherent social simulation benefits from persistent classic-AI-style scaffolding.
核心问题：LLM 能产出局部流畅的社交回合，但「下一句流畅」不等于「社交模拟连贯」。真实社交（餐厅午饭、酒店入住）是有界社交片段：有角色、脚本、物质状态、义务、承诺、时机、结束条件。
解决方案：EpisodeSim 混合架构：把经典 AI 结构作为「自然语言控制状态」交给 LLM 解读，World Master 维护共享现实、构建场景、裁决动作、追踪效果与义务、控制结束。小规模定性消融支持设计主张——LLM 流畅性提供局部质感，但连贯社交模拟需要持久的经典 AI 式脚手架组织长期行为。
对你有什么用：影游最缺的就是「场景的骨架」——不是下一句台词，而是这场戏的角色/脚本/状态/义务/何时结束。用经典 AI（状态机/脚本/世界主控）+ LLM 局部质感的混合架构，是让多角色互动场景「有结构、不崩戏」的可靠路线。

Investigating Assistant Bias in LLM User Simulators Using a Role Vector

硬数据 角色向量
Daeheon Jeong, Yoonjoo Lee, Eugene Choi, Sinie van der Ben, Juho Kim PDF ↗
LLM-based user simulators are increasingly used to evaluate autonomous agents at scale, but they exhibit 'assistant bias' — a tendency to cooperate and pursue task goals, rarely reproducing the frustration or disengagement real users show. Prior work notes this bias is baked in during training and that role-playing prompts fail to override it. The authors analyze this bias from model activations, extracting a user role vector by contrasting how the model represents user versus assistant perspectives on the same dialogue. The user direction is identifiable in activations, elicits user-like behaviors, and captures characteristics distinct from assistant traits; though activating it can exaggerate user behaviors and override individual user profiles.
核心问题：LLM 用户模拟器有「助手偏见」——倾向配合、追求任务目标，几乎不表现出真实用户的挫败、走神。角色扮演提示词压不住它，导致评测有效性受损。
解决方案：从模型激活层面分析，对比同一段对话里「用户视角 vs 助手视角」的表征，抽出「用户角色向量」。发现用户方向在激活中可识别、能引出类用户行为、与助手特质有本质区别；但过度激活会夸大用户行为、盖过个体画像。
对你有什么用：影游要让 NPC 模拟「玩家/真人」时，默认会滑向「配合型助手」。角色向量给了可量化的开关——但也提醒：强扭用户态会失真、盖掉个体人设，方向要对、力度要控。

GlossoGen: Emergent Language in Complex Multi-Agent LLM Interactions

硬数据 涌现语言
Elias Stengel-Eskin, Newton Sander, Carlos Bonetti, Sasha Boguraev, James Bowler, Hale Sirin, Simon Kirby PDF ↗
The growing rate at which LLM agents interact raises questions about language evolution in multi-LLM-agent settings. The authors introduce GlossoGen, a platform for studying multi-agent language evolution, and build the SaveVeyru scenario requiring agents with partial information to communicate under pressure. Language evolution does occur between LLM agents: the resulting languages are compositional and morphologically productive, deviate from the English prior to become incomprehensible to humans, and are driven by efficiency pressure, model strength, and a postmortem stage for agreeing on conventions. Stronger models are required for novel emergence while weaker models can learn an existing language once emerged — indicating potential for cumulative cultural evolution.
核心问题：LLM 代理之间越来越多互相交流，但它们在多代理环境里会不会「演化出新语言」、这种语言有何特性，几乎没人系统研究——涉及安全性与可监控性。
解决方案：GlossoGen 平台 + SaveVeyru 场景（部分信息代理在压力下通信）。发现 LLM 代理间确实会语言演化：生成组合性、形态有生产力的语言，偏离英语先验到人类看不懂；效率压力/模型强度/「事后复盘」阶段是演化关键；强弱模型混合群体能发展出超越最低公分母的能力（累积文化演化）。
对你有什么用：影游做「NPC 之间」的自动对话/群体生态，要警惕它们演化出「玩家看不懂的暗语」——这是双刃剑：既可能是失控隐患，也可能是「自成世界的真实感」。可控性上，加「复盘对齐」阶段能让语言收敛。
