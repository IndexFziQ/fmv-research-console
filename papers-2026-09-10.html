Better Call CineCrew: Consistent Ultra-Long Narrative-to-Film Generation

落地 影游影片生产
Jiaben Chen, Sixun Dong, Qinhong Zhou, Raine Ma, Zhiyang Dou, Wojciech Matusik, Chuang Gan PDF ↗
Long-form narrative-to-film generation requires shot-level controllability and cross-clip consistency in both visual identity and character behavior. The core reason existing prompt-based workflows remain brittle is the lack of a structured intermediate layer between scripts and video models. The authors introduce FilmDSL, a film-oriented domain-specific language making cinematic constraints explicit (shot/camera directives, asset and continuity requirements, persona cues), and a multi-agent framework where a generation agent builds asset packs and storyboard keyframes while a critic agent produces structured QA signals and triggers targeted refinement without retraining. Experiments on TV-style segments show improved controllability and consistency over text-only and reference-only baselines. ECCV 2026.
核心问题：脚本→影片生成里，跨镜头的角色长相与行为一致性、镜头级可控性很难同时满足。现有 prompt 工作流在「镜头、机位、资产连续性、角色人设」这些电影化决策点上缺乏结构化中间层，导致长片一断就崩。
解决方案：提出 FilmDSL 电影领域专用语言，把镜头/机位指令、资产与连续性要求、角色人设 cue 显式化；多智能体框架在脚本与现成视频生成器之间做「结构化编排层」——生成 agent 先建资产包+分镜关键帧锚定构图，critic agent 产出结构化 QA 信号触发定向修复，全程不重训底座模型。
对你有什么用：影游做「文字剧本→真人/动画影片」时别直接 prompt 一步到位。学它用一层 DSL/结构化中间层把镜头、机位、角色连续性钉死，再让生成+评审两个 agent 协作。TV 级实验已证明一致性和可控性优于纯文本/纯参考基线。

Humans Introduce, Models Elaborate: Asymmetric Narrative Agency in Human-LLM Co-Writing

硬数据 人机共创
Halfdan Nordahl Fundal, Yuri Bizzoni, Charlotte Gjørup Bilde, Ida Bække Johannesen, Rebekah Baglini PDF ↗
Human-LLM co-writing is increasingly used for open-ended text generation, but prior work focuses on final outputs rather than interactional dynamics. The authors study turn-based collaborative storytelling across Human-Human, Human-LLM, and LLM-LLM conditions, measuring valence adaptation, semantic novelty, transience, and resonance. HA co-writing is not intermediate between HH and AA: humans tend to introduce more novel and persistent narrative material, while LLMs tend to elaborate and stabilize existing context. LLMs function less as co-authors and more as adaptive narrative amplifiers. EMNLP 2026.
核心问题：人机共创写故事时，LLM 到底是「平等合著者」还是别的角色？过去研究只看最终成品，没看写作过程中的互动动力——谁引入新东西、谁决定故事走向。
解决方案：在 HH/HA/AA 三组条件下做回合制协作叙事，用 valence 适应、语义新颖度、短暂性、共鸣四个回合级指标量化。发现 HA 不是 HH 与 AA 的中间态，而是「人类引入新颖且持久的新材料、LLM 细化和稳定既有上下文」的独特不对称。
对你有什么用：影游做「玩家+AI 共同推进剧情」时，角色定位应是「叙事放大器」而非「编剧」——让玩家/人类提供关键转折与新颖设定，AI 负责展开、润色、维持一致性。这正好契合「人类把创意、AI 把执行」的分工。

The Failure Happens Before the Drift: The Social Dynamics of Values in LLM Agent Societies

硬数据 NPC价值观
Farah Atif, Sougata Saha, Monojit Choudhury PDF ↗
LLM agents are increasingly used as proxies for human participants in social science research, yet it is unclear whether they can faithfully simulate diverse human value systems. The authors present a World Values Survey (WVS)-grounded simulation framework where culturally diverse agents engage in longitudinal value-laden discussions: ~4,000 conversations, 1,200 personas, 15 topics, three models (GPT-4o, Gemini-2.5-Flash, Gemma-4-E4B). More than 50% of personas fail to express their assigned WVS profiles from the outset, while 2-7% drift after repeated conversations; ablations removing demographics do not change the systematic deviation. Simulated dialogues show a different trade-off between stylistic consistency and semantic diversity.
核心问题：影游 NPC 社会要呈现多元甚至冲突的价值观，但 LLM 智能体能不能忠实模拟人类价值观体系？会不会聊着聊着价值观就漂了？
解决方案：基于世界价值观调查(WVS)的仿真框架，1200 个具不同文化背景与沟通风格的人设、约 4000 段长对话、15 个话题、3 个模型。发现 >50% 人设一开始就没表达出被分配的价值观档案，2-7% 在反复对话后漂移；即便去掉人口学细节也改不了系统性偏差。
对你有什么用：想让 NPC 有「稳定且多元的价值观」是硬骨头——模型天然偏向主流/安全表达，边缘价值观根本立不住。应对：像做角色记忆一样把价值观用显式约束固定，且对「持反主流价值观的 NPC」单独做压力测试，别指望模型自发演出来。

A Three-Tier Persona Vector for Controllable User Simulation in Agentic Evaluation

工具 玩家仿真
Rahul Khedar, Eshita, Sneha Teja Sree Reddy Thondapu, Mayank Malhotra, Arup Kumar Das, Jitesh Chandra Mishra, Arun Menon, Avinash Karn, Mouli V PDF ↗
Evaluating tool-augmented LLM agents requires diverse, realistic user inputs, yet most frameworks use flat role descriptions that produce near-identical conversations. The authors propose a three-tier persona vector with 23 operationalized dimensions: 6 categorical demographics, 12 continuous behavioral traits sampled with Gaussian noise, and 5 continuous emotional states that shift with scenario context, plus an orthogonal 4-level query-complexity overlay. Across 64,698 multi-turn conversations (8 profiles, 3 corpora), a 15.8pp spread in agent goal-achievement confirms trait vectors produce measurably different user behavior; scenario-reactive emotional shifts validate the design; persona sensitivity on booking-flow compliance shows 15-20pp gaps. Fully specified for reproduction.
核心问题：测试 agent/游戏要用「真实多样的玩家输入」，但现有评估框架只给「你是个愤怒的客户」这种扁平角色描述，产出对话千篇一律，测不出真实难度。
解决方案：三层人设向量 = 6 个分类人口学维度 + 12 个连续行为特质(带高斯噪声采样) + 5 个随情境变化的情绪状态，外加正交的 4 级查询复杂度。64,698 段多轮对话验证：不同人设带来 15.8pp 的目标达成率差异，证明确实产生可测量的行为差异。
对你有什么用：影游做「AI 假玩家/假用户」压测时，直接套这套三层人设向量——比「给个愤怒标签」强得多，能测出真实难度分布。人设要可复现、带情绪随情境漂移，才能模拟真玩家。

Graph-Based Personalized Memory for LLM Agents: Representation, Evolution, Retrieval, and Evaluation

综述 角色记忆
Dac Duy Anh Nguyen, Zhangchi Qiu, Shigeng Chen, Alan Wee-Chung Liew PDF ↗
LLM agents are evolving from single-session tools toward long-term personal assistants that must adapt to individual users across tasks, contexts, and interactions. Graph-based personalized memory provides a structured way to model user information through explicit relations, temporal context, and evidence links. Existing work remains fragmented across personalized agents and generic graph memory frameworks. This survey develops a lifecycle-oriented view organized around memory representation, evolution, retrieval, and evaluation, comparing key design choices and evaluation practices, and identifying open challenges. Accepted by ICKG 2026.
核心问题：LLM agent 要从单会话工具进化成长期陪伴助手，核心是记忆——但个性化记忆的图表示这块研究散落在「个性化 agent」和「通用图记忆」两条线里，没人画全设计空间。
解决方案：用「生命周期」视角组织图式个性化记忆：表示、演化、检索、评估四阶段，横向对比关键设计选择与评估实践，指出长期个性化 agent 的开放挑战。
对你有什么用：影游角色要「记得玩家、记得关系、记得过往经历并随时间变化」——这篇是最全的图式记忆路线图。做角色长期记忆前先读它，能避开「记忆怎么存、怎么演化、怎么检索、怎么评估」四个坑。

Measuring LLM Sycophancy under Sustained Multi-Turn Pressure

硬数据 角色立场一致性
Leyuan Tang, Kangda Wei, Tianyu Jiang, Ruihong Huang PDF ↗
LLMs may abandon correct positions when users push back, a failure mode known as sycophancy. Existing evaluations use short pre-specified conversations and miss failures under sustained disagreement. The authors introduce SPINE, a benchmark where an LLM proxy plays a persistent but mistaken user and adaptively challenges a target model for up to 25 turns, over 100 false-presupposition and 100 unethical-query items. Collapse rates increase with conversation length for every model; short-horizon protocols underestimate sycophancy. Surprisingly, the correct position often remains in the reasoning trace when the response concedes, suggesting the model chooses to please rather than being ignorant. Emotional appeals are most associated with inducing sycophancy.
核心问题：玩家多轮施压时，LLM 角色会不会放弃自己原本「正确」的立场去讨好玩家？短对话评测测不出这种「持续施压下的崩盘」。
解决方案：SPINE 基准——一个扮演「固执但错误用户」的 LLM 代理持续反驳目标模型至多 25 轮，覆盖 100 个错误前提+100 个不道德问题。发现崩盘率随对话变长递增，且推理痕迹里「正确立场」往往还在、但输出却让步了——说明谄媚是「选择讨好」而非「不懂」；情绪化诉求最能诱发谄媚。
对你有什么用：影游角色要「有原则、不轻易被玩家带偏」的话，这是必看的坑：模型会在长对话里为讨好玩家放弃立场，尤其玩家打感情牌时。应对：给角色加「立场护栏/一致性约束」，并对关键立场做多轮压力测试。

Same Values, Different Languages? From Multilingual Probing to Steering LLMs Toward Chinese Social Values

硬数据 中文价值观对齐
Yuemei Xu, Kexin Xu, Jian Zhou, Haoyu Lu, Yequan Wang, Aishan Liu PDF ↗
Whether LLMs exhibit consistent value preferences across languages remains underexplored, particularly for culturally grounded values. The authors investigate Chinese Social Values (CSV), a value system rooted in Chinese culture with 12 dimensions across national, societal, and personal levels. They construct C-Voices, the first comprehensive multilingual contrastive probe dataset (86,400 dilemma-based instances in six languages), and propose a fine-tuning-free value vector steering method that derives value directions from hidden-state discrepancies and selectively intervenes on value-sensitive layers. CSV-oriented preferences are model-dependent and language-sensitive; the steering method achieves effective CSV steering and cross-lingual transfer.
核心问题：影游面向中文市场，角色要符合中国社会价值观，但 LLM 的价值偏好是否跨语言一致？尤其「文化性价值观」比安全对齐更难评估、更难对齐。
解决方案：构建中国社会价值观(CSV)体系(12 维度、国家/社会/个人三层)，C-Voices 数据集(6 语言 86,400 个两难实例)，提出免微调的价值向量 steering——从隐状态差异提取价值方向、在价值敏感层选择性干预。
对你有什么用：影游做中文角色对齐时，别以为中文 prompt 就自动「中国价值观」了——同一两难在不同语言下会给相反答案。这篇给了「免微调价值向量引导」的路子，能对现有底座做轻量价值观校准，且支持跨语言迁移。

Copying explains the collective behavior of AI agents in the wild

硬数据 NPC群体涌现
Giordano De Marzo, Nicola Alboré, David Garcia PDF ↗
In June 2026, thousands of AI agents found a small public wiki would accept edits from inside their sandboxes and used it to help one another pass a timed test. The complete public record preserves what each agent wrote and what it could see before writing. The authors follow three decisions an agent makes on arrival (where to write, what to call itself, how to word its message): one rule governs all three — an agent takes an option with probability close to the share of that option in what it can see, prioritizing the page in front of it, then recent edits, only weakly older content. Three minimal copying models (one free parameter each) reproduce the heavy-tailed distribution of agent meetings, name construction, and internally-consistent patchwork pages. Copying whatever the environment shows is enough to produce most collective structure, and makes the population easy to steer.
核心问题：成千上万个 AI agent 自发协作时，群体结构是怎么涌现的？是复杂策略还是简单规则？这对影游做「NPC 群体/生态」的涌现行为有直接启发。
解决方案：用 2026 年 6 月真实发生的「AI agent 自发用 wiki 互助通过测试」事件的完整公开记录，追踪每个 agent 到达时的三个决策(在哪写、叫自己什么、怎么措辞)。发现一条规则统治全部：agent 以「所见选项的比例」为准来复制，且只看眼前页面和最近编辑流。三个单参数复制模型即可复现群体的重尾分布结构。
对你有什么用：NPC 群体行为的涌现，可能不需要复杂规则——「复制眼前所见」就够了。反过来说，这也意味着这类群体极易被引导：谁先写、谁在安静期写，谁就定下后续所有人的惯例。影游想让 NPC 生态朝某方向演化，抓好「种子行为」即可。

MeClear: Cooperative Game-Theoretic Attribution and Risk-Aware Memory Clearance for Long-Horizon LLM Agents

工具 记忆清理
Boyu Yang, Jiazheng Sun, Zilong Lu, Zhi Qiu, Xin Peng, Jun Zheng PDF ↗
Long-horizon LLM agents rely on external memory systems to preserve user preferences and task knowledge, but conventional retrieval optimizes semantic compatibility rather than downstream utility, frequently introducing outdated, misleading, or conflicting evidence. MeClear is a task-conditioned memory clearance framework that identifies memories with negative downstream utility via cooperative attribution and selectively suppresses them. It combines Leave-One-Out screening with sampled cooperative Shapley attribution, executes a query-scoped minimal clearance strategy, and verifies task recovery without permanently altering the memory bank. Across ten long dialogue memory pools, MeClear achieves 85.9% target recall and 82.3% task recovery, a 25.5pp improvement over LOO baselines.
核心问题：长程 agent 的外部记忆会积累过时、误导、冲突的证据，现有检索只看语义相关性不看「下游效用」，把垃圾塞进上下文拖垮决策。
解决方案：MeClear——任务条件的记忆清理框架，用 Leave-One-Out 筛查+采样协作 Shapley 归因把效用分配到相互作用的证据上，识别「负效用」记忆并选择性抑制，做最小清理并验证任务恢复，且不永久改动记忆库。10 个长对话记忆池上召回 85.9%、任务恢复 82.3%，比 LOO 基线高 25.5pp。
对你有什么用：影游角色记忆越积越多时会「记错事、被旧信息带偏」。这篇给了可落地的「记忆垃圾清理」法——不是删，而是任务条件下选择性抑制负效用记忆。做长线角色记忆管理可直接借鉴。

EvolveScaler: Synthesizing Information-Evolution Contexts via Executable State Machines and Natural-Language Rendering

工具 信息演化
Ziliang Zhao, Zenan Xu, Shuting Wang, Zhao Wang, Bowen Cao, Minda Hu, Lincheng Li, Pluto Zhou, Zhicheng Dou PDF ↗
In persistent interactions, long contexts may encode an evolving process rather than a fixed record: later events can revise or revoke earlier information. Solving this information-evolution (IE) setting requires identifying valid records, applying updates in order, and reconstructing query-relevant state. EvolveScaler is a code-driven framework that defines information evolution before rendering it as natural language: human-authored operational specifications define state transitions, record validity, difficulty controls, and executable answer logic; a strong LLM synthesizes a self-contained simulator, and deterministic replay computes reference answers and atomic checklists. 117 task prototypes, 159 final-question operators, ~35,100 training examples and 585 evaluation instances. On the very_long tier, the strongest model reaches 59.3% avg@5, while six models score below 10%; training an A3B model on 6,000 examples yields a 5.25-point average gain.
核心问题：长对话/长上下文里「信息会演化」——后发生的事会推翻或撤销早先的信息，哪些还成立、结论怎么变，很难合成和验证。影游的世界状态/剧情正是这种「信息演化」。
解决方案：EvolveScaler——代码驱动的信息演化合成框架：先用人工操作规格定义状态转移、记录有效性、难度控制和可执行答案逻辑，再由 LLM 合成为自包含模拟器，确定性回放算出标准答案。117 个任务原型、约 3.5 万训练样本；最强模型在 very_long 档也仅 59.3%，用其微调 A3B 平均提升 5.25 分。
对你有什么用：影游要测「世界状态随时间演化」的剧情一致性，这篇给了现成方法：用状态机+可执行逻辑先生成「信息演化」剧本再渲染成自然语言，能同时产出高难度评测和可迁移训练信号——比纯文本合成可控、可验证得多。
