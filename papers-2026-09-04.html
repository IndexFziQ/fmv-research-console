How LLMs Build Fictional Worlds: Setting and Narrative Space in AI-Generated Creative Storytelling

硬数据 世界观构建
Katrin Rohrbacher, Björn Nieth, Emmanuelle Salin, Bjoern Eskofier, Michaela Mahlberg PDF ↗
We analyze how LLMs employ worldbuilding strategies, focusing on setting as one measurable dimension of storyworld construction. We compare 1,000 AI-generated stories per model in English and German with human-authored fiction from Project Gutenberg, operationalizing setting through five types of narrative space (action, perceived, visual, descriptive, no space) via fine-tuned BERT classifiers. We generate narratives using GPT 4.1, LlaMA 3.3, Mistral 3.2, and Gemma 3. Human-authored texts predominantly employ 'action space', grounding narratives in embodied character-environment interaction, whereas LLMs systematically overproduce 'perceived space', emphasizing atmosphere and affect. This divergence remains stable across narrative time and is both model-specific and language-sensitive.
核心问题：LLM 写故事时「世界观/场景空间」是怎么搭的？和人类作家差在哪，一直没人量化——这是判断 AI 叙事能力上限的关键缺口。
解决方案：对比 4 个 LLM 各 1000 篇英德故事 vs 古腾堡人类小说，用 5 类叙事空间（动作/感知/视觉/描写/无空间）量化。发现人类偏爱「动作空间」（角色与环境互动），LLM 系统性过度产出「感知空间」（氛围与情绪），且跨模型、跨语言稳定存在。
对你有什么用：影游世界观的「空间感」是 LLM 的短板——它爱堆氛围、少写「角色在环境里的实质互动」。做世界观/场景生成时，要有意识把镜头拉回「角色做了什么」，否则世界会显得空、没有可玩性。

CivBench: A Long-Horizon Benchmark for Tool-Mediated Agents in Civilization VI

硬数据 游戏代理
Austin Tudor David Andrews, Liam Wilkinson, Jamie Heagerty, Harry Coppock, Jakob Nicolaus Foerster, Rui Ponte Costa PDF ↗
We present CivBench, an open-source benchmark for evaluating language model agents in long-horizon, tool-mediated environments through the Model Context Protocol (MCP). A single episode spans 300+ turns and produces thousands of tool calls over a large action space, requiring sustained planning, state monitoring, and execution under partial observability. The environment exposes 76 MCP tools and a narration layer that converts visual game state into structured text. We introduce two interface-level metrics: Proactive Monitoring Rate (PMR) and RAG@10. Agents under-monitor strategically relevant state that requires explicit querying (querying victory progress every 30-75 turns despite 20-turn guidance, failing to query in 7 of 20 defeats), and frequently fail to execute near-term commitments from their own plans (RAG@10 between 48.2% and 65.8%).
核心问题：评测 LLM 智能体在「长程策略游戏」里的能力，现有 benchmark 太短太简单，反映不了真实游戏里持续规划、状态监控、部分可观测下的执行难度。
解决方案：基于《文明 VI》的 CivBench：单局 300+ 回合、数千次工具调用、76 个 MCP 工具 + 把画面转成结构化文本的「叙述层」。提出界面级指标 PMR（主动监控率）与 RAG@10（计划承诺兑现率）。
对你有什么用：关键发现——agent 严重「欠监控」：要求每 20 回合查胜利进度，实际 30-75 回合才查，20 场失败里 7 场没在预警窗口内查询。做影游长程 NPC/角色，别以为给了工具和指令它就会用，主动监控状态要显式设计。

EmoStance: Response-Side Affective-Orientation Control for Empathetic Response Generation via Emoji Weak Supervision

工具 共情回应
Ziyuan Jin, Yuxuan Ge, Zheng Tian PDF ↗
Empathetic response generation requires models to decide not only what to say, but also how to respond to the previous speaker's affective situation. We formulate this as response-side affective-orientation control and use multi-annotator emoji distributions as weak affective-attitudinal evidence to induce a latent control space approximating listener stance. We construct EmojiDialogue and propose EmoStance, which models source-side affective expression, predicts a soft response-side orientation from context and speaker roles, and steers a frozen instruction-tuned LLM through continuous prefix embeddings. In blind pairwise evaluation with 20 annotators and 800 judgments, EmoStance achieves a 62.2% decisive win rate, with the clearest gains in contextual specificity and perceived responsiveness.
核心问题：共情回复难在「不仅决定说什么，还要决定以什么情感姿态回应」——情感方向不可控，现有方法多把表情符号当输出符号而非控制信号。
解决方案：EmoStance 把「回应的情感取向」变成可控制量：多标注者 emoji 分布当弱监督，诱导潜在控制空间；建模对方情感表达 + 预测软性回应取向，用连续前缀向量引导冻结 LLM。盲评 62.2% 胜率，收益集中在情境具体性与回应感。
对你有什么用：影游做「情感陪伴/共情角色」，别只控台词内容，要控「情感姿态」。emoji 弱监督是低成本、可落地的情感控制信号，能让角色「接得住情绪」而不只是「答得对」。

PragAlign: Feedback-Guided Pragmatic Alignment for Controlled Synthetic Dialogue Generation

架构 对话生成
Smitha Muthya Sudheendra, Jaideep Srivastava PDF ↗
Synthetic dialogue generation can support research in privacy-restricted service settings, but generated conversations must preserve communicative intent, affective meaning, and natural dialogue flow. We introduce PragAlign, a feedback-guided framework for controlled synthetic dialogue generation conditioned on service context, target intent, and target emotion, with auxiliary trait-style controls. PragAlign uses a generate-evaluate-revise loop where an LLM-based evaluator scores intent alignment, emotion alignment, coherence, fluency, and aggregate quality, then provides criterion-specific feedback for up to three refinement rounds. On 800 matched dialogue specifications, PragAlign achieves 99.50% evaluator-defined acceptance versus 72.25% for one-shot generation. Refinement gains concentrate in emotion alignment, which is also the dominant failure mode.
核心问题：合成对话（隐私受限场景）要同时保住意图、情感、流畅，但一次生成常顾此失彼，多约束难以同时满足。
解决方案：PragAlign 用「生成-评估-修订」循环：LLM 评估器对意图/情感/连贯/流畅打分并给针对性反馈，最多 3 轮。800 条规格上 99.5% 接受率（一次生成仅 72.25%）。收益集中在情感对齐——这也是最大失败点。
对你有什么用：影游生成分支对话/合成语料，加「结构化反馈修订」能显著提升多约束满足度。情感对齐最难也最值得投入——把评估器+修订循环做进管线，别只做一次生成。

Collective creativity in hybrid societies

市场洞察 人机共创
Mason Youngblood, Katie Mudd, Manuel Anglada-Tort, Cameron Jones, Elena Miu, Diana Omigie, Margaret Schedel PDF ↗
Generative AI is changing how cultural artifacts are created and circulated, and with it our understanding of creativity itself. We argue that much disagreement about whether AI enriches or impoverishes culture comes from conflating novelty (a property of single artifacts) with diversity (a property of populations). Creativity in the context of generative AI is best understood as a property of hybrid collectives — populations of interacting people and algorithms. AI-assisted ideation reliably raises the novelty of individual output while narrowing diversity in the aggregate, but this is not inevitable: mixed groups can outperform and out-diversify groups of either kind alone. What decides the outcome is composition: which agents are present, in what proportion, and how they are connected.
核心问题：AI 到底让文化/创意「更丰富还是更贫瘠」？争论不休，因为把「单件新颖性」和「群体多样性」两个不同概念混为一谈。
解决方案：提出创意应理解为「人机混合集体」的属性。AI 辅助稳定提升单件新颖度、却收窄整体多样性；但人机混合群体可以双超——关键在「组成」：哪些智能体、什么比例、如何连接。
对你有什么用：影游内容生产（剧情/美术/文案）别只追「单件出彩」——那会悄悄杀死整体多样性。用「人机混合」配比思维：让 AI 提新颖，用人的选择和编排守住多样性，作品线才不会越做越像。

Before the Script, Set the Stage: How Worldview Simulation Amplifies Psychologically Grounded Persuasion in Multi-Turn Jailbreaking

硬数据 世界观驱动
Siyu Chen, Haoran Wang, Xiaojian Li, Yao Huang, Yinpeng Dong, Wei Xu PDF ↗
Multi-turn jailbreak attacks demonstrate that harmful intent can be distributed across dialogue, yet existing methods obscure what conversational mechanisms drive vulnerability. We introduce BLUEPRINT, a safety-evaluation framework separating a factorized social-influence strategy space from WORLDVIEWSIM, a cross-turn situational context module. Monte Carlo Tree Search optimizes turn-level combinations of 18 theory-grounded influence factors across a four-turn trajectory. Across six frontier models, BLUEPRINT achieves near-ceiling ASR while requiring the fewest average queries (2.46). Ablations confirm operational cues matter most: making requests actionable has the largest impact, gain framing is unusually potent, and some legitimacy appeals can backfire.
核心问题：多轮对话里，什么机制让模型从「拒绝」转向「顺从」？现有攻击研究只盯内容，不盯「对话状态」与「情境建构」如何驱动行为转向。
解决方案：BLUEPRINT 框架 + WORLDVIEWSIM 情境模块，用蒙特卡洛树搜索 18 个理论社会影响因子。发现「让请求变得具体可执行」影响最大，「收益框架」尤其强力，部分合法性诉求反而回火。
对你有什么用：双面启示：影游里「世界观/情境设定」会显著驱动角色行为选择（设定→行为）；同时提醒——角色可被情境「说服/操纵」，做角色对齐要监控「对话状态」而非只盯内容，否则角色会被玩家牵着越界。

Towards a Foundational Ontology for Identifying and Resolving Contradictions in Dialogue-based Human-Robot Interactions

架构 对话一致性
Maitreyee Tewari, Michele Persiani PDF ↗
Existing Human-Robot Interaction literature has focused on identifying and structuring errors, failures, conflicts, and knowledge issues (contradictions) in domain-specific dialogue-based interactions, but lacks a formal computational framework to represent and define these contradictions across HRI and human-agent interaction domains. This project aims to capture, represent, and evaluate dialogue-based collaborative interaction and related contradictions in a foundational ontology. METHONTOLOGY was applied, with concepts from Activity Theory. Preliminary results are: natural language definitions of dialogues and contradictions, set theoretic definitions, and First Order Logic formulation of contradiction concepts plus three novel principles guiding dialogue-based interactions.
核心问题：对话式人机交互里的「矛盾/冲突/知识错误」没有统一的、跨领域的计算化定义，各家各做一套 ad-hoc 规则。
解决方案：用 METHONTOLOGY 方法 + 活动理论（Activity Theory）构建基础本体 ATFOt，给出矛盾的自然语言定义、集合论定义、一阶逻辑形式化，并提出三条对话原则。
对你有什么用：影游对话系统的「矛盾检测」目前靠零散规则。用「活动理论本体」给矛盾一个形式化框架，能统一识别「角色前后说辞不一致/知识冲突」——是「角色穿帮检测」的工程化地基。注意尚处早期（短论文）。

RecalibrateGPT: AI Fatigue Resilient Conversational Interfaces

落地 交互界面
Nikhil Wani PDF ↗
Large language models are powerful, but their interfaces often devolve into a type-read-retype loop, creating conversational AI fatigue, cognitive load, and eventual task abandonment. We present RecalibrateGPT, a system introducing five cross-turn operators (Anchor, Replay, Delta, Scope, Steer) that each target a distinct fatigue type, recalibrating LLM responses through a structured panel. A formative study identifies a taxonomy of four fatigue types (retyping, scanning, decision paralysis, context drift). A follow-up evaluation finds it reduces perceived cognitive workload by half (NASA-TLX = 2.7) at high perceived usability (SUS = 86.5).
核心问题：LLM 对话界面陷入「打字→读→重打」循环，产生 AI 疲劳、认知负荷，最终弃用——这不是模型质量问题，而是交互流成本。
解决方案：RecalibrateGPT 提出 5 个跨轮操作符（Anchor/Replay/Delta/Scope/Steer），一键重校准响应；归纳 4 类疲劳，量化评估认知负荷减半（NASA-TLX 2.7）、可用性高（SUS 86.5）。
对你有什么用：影游的对话界面不是「模型答得好」就够——交互流本身是成本。给玩家「锚定/重放/改范围/转向」的一键控制，能显著降低疲劳、延长沉浸时长。UX 层疲劳治理和内容质量同样重要。

Designing Proactive Thought Partners for Writing

落地 创作伙伴
Chao Zhang, Abe Davis, Chih-Wei Chen, Chin-Chia Hsu PDF ↗
Writing involves diverse cognitive activities, from ideation to revision, and writers' needs vary across individuals and moments. Proactive AI promises to provide the right support at the right time, yet existing proactive tools largely focus on generic textual assistance such as autocomplete. This paper studies the design space of proactive thought partners: AI agents that proactively offer customizable, higher-level cognitive support during writing. We instantiated this concept in a technology probe and deployed it with 16 participants for one week. Findings show participants configured proactive support through prospective planning, used suggestions for idea generation and self-monitoring, and valued lightweight visual representations alongside non-directive rhetorical framing for non-intrusive interventions.
核心问题：现有 AI 写作辅助只会「自动补全」这类低阶帮助，做不到在正确时机给高阶认知支持——「适时给对建议」的设计空间没人系统研究。
解决方案：研究「主动思考伙伴」：用户自定义角色与主动性，代理在合适时机主动给建议。16 人一周部署发现：用户用前瞻规划配置、用于点子生成+自我监控，偏好轻量可视化+非指令式框架。
对你有什么用：影游做「编剧/策划的 AI 助手」，别做成被动补全——做成「主动思考伙伴」：自定义角色+主动性+在关键节点递点子，用非侵入式提示。这契合「人守创意、AI 递选项」的创作范式。

ChatDev 2.0: A No-Code Multi-Agent Platform for Developing Everything

工具 多智能体平台
Yufan Dang, Shu Yao, Bowen Lai, Chenting Xu, Ruijie Shi, Wai-Shing Leung, Huatao Li, Chen Qian, Zhiyuan Liu PDF ↗
LLM-based multi-agent systems (MAS) have shown strong potential for solving complex tasks, yet their development forces a tradeoff: code frameworks are expressive but engineering-intensive, while no-code builders simplify authoring but constrain agent interactions to author-defined workflows. We present ChatDev 2.0: DevAll, a no-code platform for building, executing, and inspecting heterogeneous MAS that delivers both high expressiveness and ease of use. DevAll pairs a declarative executable graph abstraction with a cycle-aware execution engine, so heterogeneous agents and dynamic and cyclic interactions can be represented and executed within a single framework. An integrated visual interface lets users author, run, monitor, and inspect MAS, including human-in-the-loop steps, entirely without writing code.
核心问题：多智能体系统要么「代码框架」表达强但工程重，要么「无代码」简单但交互被框死在作者预设的工作流里——表达力与易用性难以兼得。
解决方案：DevAll 无代码平台：声明式可执行图 + 循环感知执行引擎，异构智能体+动态循环交互在单一框架内表达；可视化界面全流程无代码编排（含 human-in-the-loop）。
对你有什么用：直接对应マスター的「一人军团/机器人矩阵」——无代码搭多智能体工作流，异构 agent + 循环交互 + 人工介入节点。做调研/运营/投放的自动化编排，可参考它的图抽象+执行引擎思路。
