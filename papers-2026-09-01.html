Acquire, Repair, Preserve: A Diagnosis-Guided Post-Training Recipe for Small-Model Dialogue Game Agents

工具 对话游戏
Nan Li PDF ↗
Interactive dialogue games test whether a model carries state across turns, interprets feedback, and chooses valid actions under changing constraints. Studying a 2B open-weight model in the LM Playschool Challenge, the author finds many failures are local decision failures (repeated guesses, malformed actions, violating just-seen feedback) rather than broad knowledge gaps. A three-step recipe—acquire broad participation via SFT, repair verifiable failures with turn-local preference pairs, preserve general capability—raises clemscore from 10.67 to 38.92.
核心问题：小模型玩对话游戏，卡点不是「知识不够」，而是「局部决策失败」——重复猜、动作格式错、刚收到的反馈转头就违反。
解决方案：「获取-修复-保留」三步微调：SFT 先学会参与游戏，再用回合内偏好对修复可机械验证的错误，最后保住通用能力。官方评测 clemscore 从 10.67 提到 38.92。
对你有什么用：2B 小模型也能撑起对话游戏，杠杆在「修可验证的错」而非堆数据。影游要做轻量端侧 NPC 时，这是低成本落地样板。

Stranger, Fan, or Peer? A Systematic Study on the Role of Interlocutor in Persona-Based Dialogue Generation

硬数据 人设可见性
Daniela Occhipinti, Malvina Nissim, Marco Guerini PDF ↗
Persona-based dialogue is usually conditioned on the speaker's biography, but who sees whose biography can vary across training, inference, and evaluation—a three-stage factorisation prior work treats as one factor. The authors toggle biography visibility separately across stages and use an LLM judge for author identification. They find training-time visibility (more than inference-time) determines whether a model expresses persona traits or copies biographical text; models trained with interlocutor visibility copy less; asymmetric disclosure leaks target biography into interlocutor turns.
核心问题：角色对话里，到底谁「看得见」谁的人设卡，会悄悄决定角色是「演出来」还是「背出来」——这个三阶段因子过去被当成单一变量忽略了。
解决方案：把「训练/推理/评测」三阶段的人设可见性拆开做因子实验，用 LLM 作者识别量化。发现训练期可见性才是决定角色「表达人设 vs 照抄传记」的关键。
对你有什么用：写影游 NPC 人设卡时要显式规定「训练期谁可见谁的设定」，否则角色会把传记原文背出来。对话对象是陌生人/粉丝/同辈，角色说话方式也不一样。

The Shape of Power: A Multilingual Framework for Social Power Reasoning in Dialogues

硬数据 权力关系
Farah Atif, Sougata Saha, Monojit Choudhury PDF ↗
Social power shapes human interaction, yet computational studies of power remain narrow. The authors build the first extensible multilingual framework for social power reasoning, grounded in social-science schema, a native-speaker annotation pipeline, and a scalable interface. They annotate 15,836 instances from 100 scenes of French and Egyptian Arabic movies, and evaluate six LLMs on cross-cultural social power reasoning, finding persistent gaps in relational and theory-of-mind reasoning.
核心问题：对话里的「权力关系」（谁压谁、谁求谁）是戏剧张力的核心，但计算模型几乎没系统研究过，跨文化更是空白。
解决方案：从电影剧本建了首个多语言社会权力推理框架：社科理论 schema + 母语者标注管线，覆盖 100 场景 15,836 条标注，测了 6 个 LLM 的跨文化权力推理能力。
对你有什么用：影游人物关系本质是权力关系。要让角色「会看眼色、懂谁压谁」，需要显式建模社会权力——电影剧本是最现成的训练语料。

First Make It Playable, Then Make It Good: Staged Interaction Learning for Small Dialogue-Game Agents

工具 小模型
Syed Mahbubul Huq, Pranava Madhyastha PDF ↗
The authors present Qwen-GuidePlay-2B, a 2B model for dialogue-game interaction, fine-tuned from Qwen3.5-2B in three stages: SFT on only successful trajectories, weighted turn-level SFT, and teacher-guided SFT (the teacher only fixes formatting and evaluates, never creates gold actions). It scores 57.12 clemscore, the second-highest delta among submissions. Full-trajectory imitation drives playability; turn-level and teacher-guided training improve decision-making; replay-repair and hard-example mining did not help.
核心问题：小模型对话游戏 agent 怎么做才「先能玩、再玩好」？很多方法堆复杂流程，收益却甚微。
解决方案：三阶段：① 只在成功轨迹上 SFT；② 加权回合级 SFT；③ 教师模型只修格式、不打新金标。结论：模仿完整轨迹提升可玩性，回合级/教师监督提升决策。
对你有什么用：小模型做游戏 agent 的杠杆不在「复杂重放修复」，而在「精心挑数据」。先保可玩、再抠决策——正是影游 MVP 的工程节奏。

Preserving General Capabilities during Domain Specialization with Uncertainty-Calibrated MOPD

架构 角色微调
Ziyuan Liu, Jiao Ou, Jian Liang, Ruiming Tang, Cheng Luo PDF ↗
Specializing LLMs to a vertical domain degrades general capabilities (reasoning, coding, instruction following, creative writing). The authors study this trade-off in Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation and propose uncertainty-calibrated MOPD: dual-temperature sampling broadens the candidate pool, positive-advantage-density filtering selects strong learning signals, and centered log-likelihood filtering retains updates by direction-endorsement consistency. On role-playing and medical specialization, general capability improves 4.73% and 10.84% over standard MOPD.
核心问题：把模型专精到角色扮演后，通用能力（推理、写作、指令遵循）会掉——这是所有「角色微调」的通病。
解决方案：不确定性校准的多教师在线蒸馏：双温度采样扩大候选池、正优势密度过滤选强学习信号、中心化对数似然做方向-认可一致性。角色扮演场景通用能力较标准 MOPD 再提 4.73%。
对你有什么用：影游角色模型微调别把通用能力调没了。这套「边专精边保通用」的方法，让角色模型不会一专精就「变傻」。

Entity-Memory Graph Retrieval Improves Evidence Coverage in Long-Conversation Question Answering

架构 实体记忆
Shumao Sun PDF ↗
Entity-Memory graph retrieval keeps dialogue turns as verbatim Memory nodes, links repeated mentions through shared Entities, and connects adjacent Memories with directed chronological edges. At query time, retrieval moves from entity gating through semantic fusion and one-hop chronological recovery to dense backfill. On 1,986 questions from ten LoCoMo conversations, graph retrieval raises official evidence recall at top-k 25 from 79.75% to 84.48%.
核心问题：长对话问答里，纯密集向量检索会漏掉「同一实体反复出现」的相邻记忆，证据覆盖不足。
解决方案：实体记忆图：回合存成逐字 Memory 节点，共享实体连接反复提及，时间顺序边连接相邻记忆，检索走「实体门控→语义融合→单跳时序恢复→稠密回填」。证据召回 top-k25 从 79.75% 提到 84.48%。
对你有什么用：影游 NPC 的长期记忆别只靠向量检索。用「实体+时间」图把同一角色/物品的多次提及串起来，角色才记得住关键信息。

Predicting Turn-Taking Outcomes in Multi-Party Conversation: Interpretable Modelling of Speech and Gaze Dynamics with Interpersonal Closeness

落地 多人对话
Mark Dourado, Karim Haddad, Henrik G. Hassager, Stefania Serafin PDF ↗
Smooth speaker transitions require predicting when to enter the conversation. This study models how gaze and speech, plus perceived interpersonal closeness, signal conversational floor changes in free four-person dialogue. Using the GaMMA corpus, interpretable features (gaze transition motifs, entropy, addressee identity, mutual gaze, loudness, IOS closeness) predict floor-transfer as gap vs overlap, reaching ROC AUC 0.76, robust across noise conditions.
核心问题：多人对话里谁该什么时候开口——预测抢话/让话的时机，是全双工语音角色的老大难，嘈杂多人场景尤甚。
解决方案：用「视线 + 音量 + 人际亲密度」可解释特征建模 floor 转移（gap/overlap），视线+音量融合 ROC AUC 0.76，视线在噪声下依然稳健。
对你有什么用：影游要做「多人语音角色」，不能只看文本。视线和音量是判断「该谁说话」的关键信号，多人戏里尤其重要。

Multi-Expert Conformal Risk Control for Pairwise LLM Judging in Open-Ended Dialogue

工具 对话评测
Ming Cheng, Yusheng Dai, Qiuhong Ke, Zhaolin Chen, Lizhen Qu PDF ↗
The authors explore multi-expert Conformal Risk Control for pairwise LLM-as-a-judge evaluation in open-ended dialogue. Score Averaging and Decision Voting aggregate at score and decision levels; Marginal-Calibrated Conformal Consensus (MC3) aligns heterogeneous experts' distinct scoring scales via initial threshold ratios while jointly tuning a unified decision function. They construct Panel, a 1,800-pair human preference benchmark over three dialogue domains, and show consistent accuracy and acceptance-rate gains.
核心问题：开放对话质量评测，单个 LLM 打分会偏、多个 LLM 打分尺度又不一致——「谁评得准」本身不可靠。
解决方案：多专家一致性风险控制：先做分数平均/决策投票，再用 MC3 用初始阈值比对齐各专家尺度、统一决策函数保交换性，1,800 组人工偏好基准验证有效。
对你有什么用：影游要评估 NPC 对话好不好，别只信一个模型的打分。多评委 + 尺度校准，才能得到可信的对话质量分数。

The Illusion of What If: Evaluating the Breakdown of Counterfactual Reasoning in LLMs

硬数据 反事实
Yucheng Wang, Yuetian Du, Zhengyi Liu, Rongyu Zhang, Bing Zhao, Boyu Yang, Ming Kong, Lin Qu, Hu Wei, Jie Liu, Qiang Zhu PDF ↗
Counterfactual reasoning requires reasoning beyond the observed world. The authors introduce WhatIfBench, a diagnostic benchmark of 220 open-domain, long-horizon what-if questions, and PRISM, which converts explanations into a Response-Derived Semantic Causal Graph evaluated by a Process Metric and a Rubric Metric. The strongest model reaches only 64.62%, with persistent causal gaps, premise drift, and topology fragmentation.
核心问题：「如果当时…会怎样」是分支叙事的灵魂，但 LLM 的开放域反事实推理能力到底多强，一直缺可信评测。
解决方案：WhatIfBench（220 道开放 what-if 题）+ PRISM 把解释转成语义因果图、用过程度量+评分度量双评测。最强模型也仅 64.62%，存在前提漂移、因果拓扑碎片化。
对你有什么用：影游做「分支后果推演」别高估 LLM——流畅的「what-if」叙事背后，因果链常常是碎的。关键分支要人工校验因果是否成立。

CultureConverse: A Multilingual Multi-turn Simulation Harness for Culturally Grounded Assistance in East and Southeast Asia

落地 文化扎根
Bryan Chen Zhengyu Tan, Weihua Zheng, Thong T. Doan, Bich Ngoc Doan, Jia Wang Peh, Xiaoyuan Yi, Jing Yao, Xing Xie, Nancy F. Chen, et al. PDF ↗
Current cultural evaluations reduce culture to single-turn factual recall. The authors introduce CultureConverse, a scalable multilingual simulation harness for culturally grounded multi-turn assistant dialogue covering 10 East and Southeast Asian regions, 58 subgroup identities, and 7 domains. The CultureConverse-DS dataset contains 14,610 benchmark episodes and 274,295 oracle-guided dialogues. Fine-tuning on 27,860 high-quality samples improves in-domain assistance and transfers out-of-domain.
核心问题：多轮对话里的「文化扎根」被简化成单选题式的事实回忆，抓不住「用户在多轮互动中带着文化约束求助」的真实场景。
解决方案：CultureConverse 覆盖 10 个东亚/东南亚地区、58 个子群体、7 个领域，生成 14,610 条评测 + 27.4 万条金标对话；用 2.7 万条高质量样本微调即可提升域内助人并迁移到域外。
对你有什么用：影游角色做本土化，别停留在「回答文化常识」。多轮、带文化约束的对话模拟，是让 NPC「接地气、不冒犯」的落地方案。
